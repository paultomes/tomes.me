Why this exists

A record of how we thought

Families preserve photographs, letters, objects and stories. This collection is an attempt to preserve something less visible: the questions we asked, the beliefs we held, the lessons we learned and the ways our thinking changed over time.

These are not intended to be polished declarations or permanent truths. They are snapshots of people at particular moments in their lives, written in the hope that they may one day be interesting, useful or simply comforting to those who read them.
