Musings from Dad

Thoughts for Adam, Daniel, Jack and Phoebe, written as the world changes around us.

About these musings

These are not instructions, predictions or definitive answers. They are a record of how I saw the world at different moments in my life, what I believed mattered and what I hoped to pass on.

Some ideas will age well. Others may turn out to be completely wrong. My hope is that the thinking behind them remains useful, even when the conclusions change.
