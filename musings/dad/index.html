Musings from Dad

Thoughts written over time as I try to understand the world around me. Some ideas will age well and others will not. My hope is that the thinking behind them remains useful long after I have written them.

July 2026 – Present

Why these exist

These musings are letters to Adam, Daniel, Jack and Phoebe. They are not intended to be instructions or predictions, only an honest record of what I believed at different moments in my life.

Some of these ideas may prove to be wrong, and that is perfectly fine. Understanding the world means being willing to change your mind from time to time.

I do not expect to always be right. I do hope to always be curious.

About this collection

These musings began in July 2026 when I realised that the world was changing faster than at any point in my lifetime. Rather than trying to predict the future, I wanted to leave behind a record of how I thought about it while I was living through it.
